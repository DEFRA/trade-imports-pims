# User Story Catalogue

This page lists all 60 consolidated user stories in the PIMS requirements baseline, derived from 65 source records plus 9 PLNT issues added on 22 September 2026 that were missing from the original compilation, plus a further 12 new stories and amendments to 11 existing stories added following the Jira delta assessment of a 111-issue export. The PLNT issues are distributed across US-001, US-006, US-044 and the new US-049 story; PLNT-4538 is shared by US-044 and US-049, while PLNT-4536 and PLNT-4542 are also referenced by more than one story. A further 7-issue delta (EDA-308, IMTA-5887, IMTA-6185, IMTA-6357, IMTA-6720, IMTA-6950, IMTA-7423) was subsequently assessed: five were already fully consolidated into the baseline (IMTA-5887 in US-017, IMTA-6185 in US-025, IMTA-6357 in US-005, IMTA-6720 in US-046, IMTA-6950 in US-045), and two were folded in as amendments — EDA-308 (superseded by EDA-432, added to US-001/BR-045 traceability) and IMTA-7423 (new AC-9/BR-060 on US-006). A further 38-issue delta was subsequently assessed: 13 were already fully consolidated (IMTA-5862, IMTA-5886, IMTA-5888, IMTA-5890, IMTA-5892, IMTA-5933, IMTA-6255, IMTA-6372, IMTA-6668, IMTA-6669, IMTA-6680, IMTA-6699, IMTA-7201); 2 were traceability-only additions to existing content (IMTA-7368 in US-052, IMTA-7379 in US-001); 1 corrected BR-059's rollup rule and added its citation (IMTA-6032); 1 was a duplicate of an already-cited defect (IMTA-10483, same defect as IMTA-10482); 2 were noted as historical/superseded context with no new requirement (IMTA-8221, IMTA-8235, see ASM-015); 1 had no acceptance criteria to consolidate (IMTA-7781); 1 annotated an existing open dependency (IMTA-6662, see DEP-007); and the remaining 17 were folded in as new acceptance criteria across US-001, US-002, US-003, US-029, US-044, US-052, US-053 and US-058.

**Total:** 60 stories · 11 epics · 60 business rules
