    Glossary¶

    This glossary defines canonical terminology for PIMS. Where multiple terms were used inconsistently across source Jira stories, the preferred term is listed first and alternatives are noted.

    Business Terms¶

    Term Definition Aliases / Notes
    Import Record The primary case record in PIMS representing a single consignment being risk-assessed. Contains links to health certificates, place of origin, risk level and post-import check requirements. Previously called "Import Application" in some stories. Use "Import Record" as the canonical term. The underlying D365 schema and some workflow artefact names may still use importapplication / "Import Application".
    ITAHC Intra-Trade Animal Health Certificate. The EU health certificate for live animals, raised in TRACES by the EU exporter. Always written ITAHC (the H is often dropped in speech, but never in writing).
    DOCOM Commercial Document. Used for low-risk movements such as animal by-products and one equine derogation. Simpler than an ITAHC.
    CVED Common Veterinary Entry Document. A certificate used for goods imported from third countries at a Border Inspection Post.
    Import Notification A legacy notification concept used for manual or non-TRACES pre-notifications (for example IV66-related notifications). Distinct from Importer Notification. The current implementation evidence does not show a separate dedicated Import Notification entity in solution metadata. The wording persists in some labels, views and dashboard text.
    Importer Notification A notification submitted by an importer via IPAFFS and automatically received by PIMS. Created by IMTA-5864 / IMTA-7201. This is the implemented D365 entity for IPAFFS-originated notifications. Some legacy labels and options may still use the older term "Import Notification".
    Post Import Check A physical or document-based inspection of a consignment conducted after it has entered the UK. May be triggered automatically by PIMS business rules or manually by a caseworker. Also referred to as "Post Import Inspection" in informal usage. Use "Post Import Check" as the canonical term.
    Place of Origin A registered premises from which consignments originate. PIMS maintains a Trust Level (Gold or Bronze) for each Place of Origin based on inspection history. Also referred to as "Premises of Origin" in some stories. Use "Place of Origin" as the canonical term.
    Trust Level The risk rating assigned to a Place of Origin. Values: Gold (low risk, verified compliance history) and Bronze (standard risk, default).
    Gold Trust Level Awarded to a Place of Origin after 3 consecutive satisfactory post-import check outcomes. Results in reduced inspection frequency (1-in-10 consignments).
    Bronze Trust Level The default Trust Level assigned to all new Places of Origin. Results in higher inspection frequency.
    Lock to Bronze A manual override that prevents PIMS from automatically promoting a Place of Origin's Trust Level from Bronze to Gold.
    Import Query A formal query raised by a caseworker against an Import Record, directed to the importer or a third party. Tracked through creation, assignment and resolution.
    Match Record A system-supported matching artefact used to identify candidate related Import Records for inbound certificates or notifications. Supports matching workflows and the "Match View", including display of Work Schedule Number where available.
    Risk Level The automated risk classification applied to an Import Record. Values: P1 (high risk), P2 (medium risk / low risk commercial), P3 (low risk).
    P1 High-risk Import Record. Post-import check requirement determined by Gold/Bronze commodity rules.
    P2 Medium-risk Import Record. Subject to the 10% random post-import check coverage rule.
    P3 Low-risk Import Record. Subject to the 2% random post-import check coverage rule.
    Commodity Risk Level A configurable rule that maps a Country + Commodity Type combination to a Risk Level (P1, P2, P3). Also called "Import Country Commodity Risk Level" in early stories.
    Gold/Bronze Commodity A commodity type (and optional country list) for which the Gold/Bronze Place of Origin trust level rules apply.
    Inspection Coverage Rule A configurable rule defining the threshold count at which a random inspection must be triggered. Rules are named and associated to risk levels (e.g., "10% Rule" for P2, "2% All-Case-Random" for P3, "Gold Commodity Every X" for Gold commodities). Thresholds are stored as whole numbers (Number of Records Until Inspection) and are managed by System Administrators. US-013
    Inspection Coverage Count A counter tracking the number of Import Records processed since the last random inspection was triggered for a given coverage rule.
    Post Import Check Outcome The result of a post-import check. Values include: Satisfactory, Not Visited, Unsatisfactory, Non-Compliant, Resolved Not Required, Cancelled, Quarantined, Additional Inspection Required.
    IV65 A formal written query sent from Defra to a place of origin or consignor. Has a 14-day response due date.
    IV66 The paper Import Notification Form (form reference IV66(ES), issued under The Trade in Animals and Related Products Regulations 2011) completed by importers to notify Defra of an incoming consignment. Captures importer details, country of origin, transiting countries, date of import, place of destination, premises of origin, species/product, quantity, and port of entry. The IV66 paper process is the predecessor to digital submission via IPAFFS. In the current system, pre-notification data arrives as an Importer Notification via IPAFFS rather than through the paper form; manual Import Records may still be created for IV66 edge cases. Previously incorrectly described as "Creates an Import Notification in PIMS" — IV66 is the source form, not a PIMS record type.
    IV17 A document associated with Post Import Check processing.
    Warble Fly Treatment Declaration A declaration confirming that cattle consignments have been treated for warble fly prior to import.
    Devolved Office The regional Defra office responsible for processing an Import Record.
    APHA Region Animal and Plant Health Agency region. A geographic subdivision used to assign responsibility for post-import checks.
    Geographic Team A regional operational team: IRMS – Scotland, IRMS – Wales, IRMS – England (North, South, West). IRMS = Import Risk Management Service
    Business Process Flow (BPF) The Dynamics 365 guided process workflow applied to an Import Record (stages: Triage, Risk Assessment, Post Import Check, Completion).
    Counter History An audit record of every increment, decrement or reset of a risk assessment or trust level counter, with a reason.
